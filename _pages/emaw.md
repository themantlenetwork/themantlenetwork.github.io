The **Earth Mantle Workshop (EMAW)** aims at providing a global framework of advanced knowledge on the evolution of the Earth's mantle, accessible for early-career researchers and students.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/emaw2018_group.jpg' | relative_url }}" class="align-center" alt="">
*Group photo of EMAW 2018 in Pavia.*

## History

The workshop was originally focused on the state of knowledge on the European mantle, notably from the study of peridotite massif and xenoliths brought to the surface by Cenozoic to Quaternary alkaline lavas and kimberlites in the Fenno-Scandian–Ukrainian shield. In 2021, EMAW becomes the **Earth Mantle Workshop (EMAW)** and now aims at providing a global framework of advanced knowledge on the evolution of the Earth Mantle.

The first meeting was held in Ferrara (Italy) in 2007, organized by M. Coltorti (Earth Sciences Department, University of Ferrara), H. Downes (Birkbeck College, London University), M. Grégoire (Observatoire Midi Pyrénées, CNRS, Toulouse) and S. Y. O’Reilly (ARC National Key Centre, GEMOC, Macquarie University) and sponsored by the University of Ferrara, the *Istituto Universitario di Studi Superiori* (IUSS) of the same university, the *Gruppo Nazionale di Petrografia* (GNP) and the *Federazione Italiana di Scienze della Terra* (FIST).
