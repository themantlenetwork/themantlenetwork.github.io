The **MANTLE NETWORK Initiative** is an international collective of scientists studying the Earth’s mantle. It emerged from the EMAW and Orogenic Lherzolite communities in 2025.

# Activities

Our activities include webinars featuring petrologists, geochemists, geophysicists, and geodynamicists. We coordinate international meetings to foster collaboration across the global mantle research community.
