# Exploring the dynamic interior of our planet

Researchers are revealing how the mantle drives Earth’s continuous internal reshaping and long-term evolution.

Modern mantle science combines [geology](/geology/), [geodynamics](/geodynamics/), [geophysics](/geophysics/), [geochemistry](/geochemistry/), [petrology](/petrology/), [volcanology](/volcanology/).
