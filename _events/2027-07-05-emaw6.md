Following the five successful Earth Mantle Workshop (EMAW)
conferences held in Ferrara (2007), Wrocław (2015), Pavia
(2018), Toulouse (2022), and Vienna (2025), the next meeting
will take place in Sopron, Hungary, in 2027, hosted by the
**HUN-REN Institute of Earth Physics and Space Science**.

The international conference aims to bring together Earth
scientists with diverse scientific backgrounds and experience
from around the world, fostering the exchange of new ideas,
concepts, and techniques in the fields of mantle geology,
geochemistry, and geophysics, from the tectonic scale all
the way to the nanoscale.

In addition to providing a platform for scientific discussion,
the conference will also address topics highly relevant to
society, including research on economically important elements,
deep-rooted geohazards, and deeply sourced fluid fluxes that
can reach the Earth's surface.
