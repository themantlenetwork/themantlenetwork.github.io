The 6<sup>th</sup> SerpentineDays meeting will bring together researchers from different disciplines to discuss recent advances in serpentinite research. The programme will cover field research, sea-going expeditions, experimental investigations and computational modelling.

Topics will cover serpentinization and life, ocean-floor serpentinites, their role in subduction zones and ophiolites, serpentinization on planetary bodies, and applications such as natural hydrogen resources and CO₂ sequestration.

The meeting will take place at the **Centre for Deep Sea Research, MARUM, University of Bremen**. Participants will also have the opportunity to visit the Bremen Core Repository and its collection of serpentinite drill cores.

The preliminary programme includes plenary talks and poster sessions from 6–8 July, preceded by an icebreaker on 5 July and followed by a guided tour of the IODP core repository on 9 July.
