GLOM 2027, entitled *Oceanic Lithosphere: From Ophiolite to Ridge — and Back*, will take place in Rome, Italy, on 2–3 February 2027. The workshop aims to bring together researchers studying modern oceanic lithosphere and ophiolites, providing a forum to discuss how these complementary records can contribute to our understanding of oceanic lithosphere formation and evolution.

The scientific programme will address the connections between modern oceanic processes and their preserved on-land counterparts, including the inheritance and recycling of oceanic lithosphere across successive generations. The meeting will be organized as a single-session workshop, combining four invited keynote lectures with contributed presentations and discussions.

The workshop will be held at the **Department of Earth Sciences, Sapienza University of Rome**. Participation will be limited to 120 participants, with no registration fee; a small contribution will be requested to cover coffee breaks and basic organizational expenses.
