The 13<sup>th</sup> International Kimberlite Conference (13IKC) will take place in Diamantina, Minas Gerais, Brazil, from 6 to 11 August 2028. Building on a long-standing international conference series, the meeting will bring together researchers working on kimberlites, diamonds, and related aspects of mantle geology.

The conference will cover a broad range of topics, including kimberlite and lamproite petrology, mantle geochemistry, diamond formation and properties, mineralogy, geochronology, and the geology of diamond-bearing regions. It will provide a forum for presenting recent advances, exchanging ideas, and discussing current challenges in understanding the origin, evolution, and emplacement of kimberlitic magmas and their associated mantle processes.
