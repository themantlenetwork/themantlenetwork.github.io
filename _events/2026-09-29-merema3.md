The third edition of MEREMA (MElting and fluid/melt-rock REactions in the MAntle), the International School on Mantle Petrology, will take place in Volterra, Tuscany, Italy, from 29 September to 2 October 2026. The school aims to provide an integrated overview of the structure, chemical composition, and dynamics of the Earth's mantle, bringing together perspectives from petrology, geochemistry, and geophysics.

The scientific programme will address mantle processes across a range of spatial scales, from nanoscale and microscale melt–rock interactions to global mantle dynamics. Topics will include the sampling and characterization of the Earth's mantle through xenoliths, mantle outcrops, drilling and basalt compositions, as well as experimental approaches, analytical techniques, and numerical modelling. The programme will combine lectures by invited specialists with practical sessions and poster presentations, providing opportunities for students and early-career researchers to present their work and exchange ideas.

An associated field trip, entitled *A Deep Dive into the Oceanic Mantle*, will take place in Alpine Corsica from 27 to 29 September 2026, prior to the school. The excursion will focus on the Monte Maggiore ultramafic massif, exploring mantle lithologies and melt–rock interactions within the Alpine Jurassic ophiolites.
